Build better study habits

Welcome to Student Study Hub

Keeping up with college work can be difficult when several deadlines arrive at once. This site shares simple ways to plan your time, organize notes, and prepare for exams.

Start small: choose one assignment, decide on your next step, and set aside time to work on it.

Start with Study Planning

Study Planning

  • Write down your upcoming deadlines.
  • Break large assignments into small tasks.
  • Set study times around classes and work.

Note-Taking

  • Record main ideas in your own words.
  • Try Cornell notes, outlines, or concept maps.
  • Review notes and mark questions after class.

Exam Preparation

  • Spread review across several days.
  • Practice answering questions from memory.
  • Use a checklist to track topics to review.
